Test mode · Test conditions

Vocabulary test

This works like the paper test in class. All words, in a random order, Latin to English. You type the meaning and press Next. You cannot go back, there are no hints, and you see no marks until the end.

⏱  You have 10 minutes. The test hands itself in when the clock runs out. Spelling counts — an answer with a spelling mistake is marked wrong, just as it would be on paper. Give one meaning per word — any meaning on the list is accepted, but hedging ("god or goddess") scores nothing. If a word has two meanings on the list you may write both.

Every attempt is recorded for your teacher, with the time you took and the words you missed. You may sit it more than once — the point is to get to 100% and stay there.
